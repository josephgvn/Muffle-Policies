How to mute Webex from any app on a Mac

The Webex mute shortcut only works while Webex is in front, and its controls hide when you share your screen.

Muffle mutes your microphone from anywhere on your Mac, so you can mute a Webex meeting without leaving what you're doing.

Try Muffle free for 3 daysComing soon to the Mac App Store

Mute Webex from anywhere

  1. 1

    Install Muffle and allow microphone access.

  2. 2

    Join your Webex meeting.

  3. 3

    Press Control-Option-M or your AirPods stem to mute.

  4. 4

    Use the floating mini controller to see your mic state while you share.

  • Webex may still show you as unmuted. Participants hear silence while the Muffle icon is red.
  • Turn on “Start every call muted” for large meetings.

Questions

Does Muffle work with Webex in the browser?

Yes. It mutes the microphone, so it works with any Webex client.

Can I mute Webex with AirPods?

Yes, press the stem during the meeting. The keyboard shortcut works with any headset.
