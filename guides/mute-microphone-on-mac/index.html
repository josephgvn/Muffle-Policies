How to mute your microphone on a Mac

There's no mute key for the microphone on a Mac. Depending on the app and the moment you have a few options, and only some of them work everywhere.

Your options

  1. The call app's own button or shortcut. It works only in that app, and only while that app is in front.
  2. System Settings, Sound, Input. Dragging the input level to zero isn't a real mute on every mic, and apps can raise it again.
  3. A global shortcut with Muffle. One key mutes the microphone itself, in every app.
  4. Your AirPods. With Muffle, pressing the stem mutes any call, including Google Meet in Chrome.
  • The Muffle menu bar icon turns red while the mic is off, whatever app you're in.
  • Quitting Muffle always turns your microphone back on.

Questions

Is there a built-in microphone mute on a Mac?

Not a system-wide one. macOS only mutes the mic inside apps that support it. Muffle adds a real system-wide mute.

Does muting in Muffle mute every app?

Yes. Muffle switches off every microphone at the hardware level, so no app can hear you until you unmute.
